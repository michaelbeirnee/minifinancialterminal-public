Flagged

Every screen elsewhere here measures a level. These measure a change: a company's newest filing against its own previous one — a risk factor added or dropped, a customer concentration appearing or vanishing, an auditor gone, a share count moving against the buyback, an accrual outrunning the sales behind it, a concept tagged for the first time, a wave of sell-side rating changes. Every flag is dated to the day it became public, so it grades in the same signal log as every other idea source.

Three accrual flags computed across every SEC filer from the cross-company XBRL frames — a handful of requests for a couple of thousand companies, from the filings rather than a vendor. Each row carries its percentile in the distribution the screen just computed. Off-calendar year-ends whose balance and flow frames come from different periods are dropped rather than mis-compared; the company tab reads those.

MARKET SCREEN
Pick a flag and press Run.

Quarter-over-quarter changes in reported 13F holdings, every filer aggregated per issuer from SEC's structured data set, stated in days of the name's own average volume — so at a small cap the entry or exit itself is the liquidity event, and what the sellers still hold is the forecastable part. Filers present in both quarters only; index managers' share of the flow measured; SPACs, probable PIPEs and implausible single filers set aside. A 13F is due 45 days after quarter end and the data set lands a fortnight later, so this is always the quarter before last.

FLOW SCREEN
or one symbol:
Press Run for the market, or type a symbol.

Cluster a company with the peers that disclose the same geography or product line in their revenue notes, find the lines where several of them report the same inflection in the same fiscal cohort, and name the member with real exposure to that line whose consensus has not moved. The peers' disclosures are the evidence for the laggard's claim; the laggard's own print is the catalyst. Every shared line is listed, fired or not, so you can see why one didn't.

READ-THROUGH
Type a hub symbol. The first read of a cluster opens a filing per member — half a minute cold, seconds after.

Every flag type for one or more symbols. The document flags — risk factors, concentration, the auditor cover page — read two annual reports, which is a few seconds on a cold cache and instant afterwards; the rest is one cached SEC object and one vendor call. No flags means nothing moved on these dimensions; the skipped list says which readers could not run, so silence is never mistaken for a clean bill.

SCAN
Type a symbol and press Scan.

What each flag measures, which two states it compares, where it is read from — and how it characteristically produces a false positive. The last column is the point: a reader should start from the objection, not the headline.
